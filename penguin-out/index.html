Privacy Policy for Penguin Out

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Penguin Out (“the Game”) is a mobile puzzle game developed by Efe Bostancı, Moryn Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game handles and why.

In short: the Game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. It is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID; where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first. To learn which levels are too hard, it sends anonymous play statistics to GameAnalytics. Purchases are made through Google Play. Your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally on your device. This includes:

  • The levels you have finished and the stars you earned on them
  • Your sound, music and vibration settings
  • The hints you have left, and whether you have bought ads away

This information never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Play statistics (analytics)

To see how the levels play and which ones are too hard, the Game uses GameAnalytics. While you play, it sends:

  • Which level you start, finish or leave, how many moves and seconds it took, how many tries, undos and hints, and how many stars you earned
  • Basic technical details: device model, operating system and its version, the Game's version, language, a rough location (country) derived from the connection, and session times
  • A random identifier created for this installation, so that plays from the same device can be counted together, and errors the Game runs into

This information is not linked to your name or account, is used only to improve the Game, and is not sold or used for advertising. GameAnalytics processes it on our behalf. To have it deleted, contact us at the address below.

Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads: a short video you can choose to watch for a hint, and now and then a full-screen ad between levels. The ads come from Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, basic device and app information, a rough location derived from the connection, and how you interact with the ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Depending on your consent, the ads may be personalised.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is shown, and you can change it at any time with Privacy options in the Game's settings. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings.

Buying “Remove ads” turns off the ads between levels.

Purchases

The Game sells hints and the removal of ads. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the Game which items you own, so that they can be restored on a new device.

Permissions

The Game asks for permission to vibrate the device, which it uses for short haptic feedback when you play; you can turn it off in the Game's settings. It also uses internet access, for the play statistics, ads and purchases described above, and the advertising ID permission, for ads. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with a new “last updated” date.
