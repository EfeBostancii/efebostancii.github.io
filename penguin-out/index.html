Privacy Policy for Penguin Out

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Penguin Out (“the Game”) is a mobile puzzle game developed by Efe Bostancı, Moryn Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game handles and why.

In short: the Game does not collect, share or send any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally on your device. This includes:

  • The levels you have finished and the stars you earned on them
  • Your sound, music and vibration settings

This information never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Advertising and analytics

The Game currently shows no advertisements and uses no analytics or tracking services. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing what is collected, by whom and why.

Permissions

The Game asks for permission to vibrate the device, which it uses for short haptic feedback when you play; you can turn it off in the Game's settings. The Game's engine also declares internet access, but the Game does not currently send or receive any data. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

Children

The Game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with a new “last updated” date.
