Privacy Policy for Tricky Building

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Tricky Building (“the Game”) is a mobile tower-stacking game developed by Moryn Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game handles and why.

In short: the Game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. It is free, has no in-app purchases, and shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID; where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first. We do not collect any analytics of our own. Your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally on your device. This includes:

  • Your best height, the daily challenge results and your play statistics (games played, blocks placed and similar counts)
  • The in-game coins you have earned, the themes you have unlocked and the ones you have chosen
  • Your daily missions and whether you have finished the first-game guide
  • Your sound, vibration and screen-shake settings

This information never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the Game. In-game coins have no real-money value and cannot be bought.

Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads: a short video you can choose to watch to keep playing or to earn coins, and now and then a full-screen ad after a game ends. The ads come from Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, basic device and app information, a rough location derived from the connection, and how you interact with the ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Depending on your consent, the ads may be personalised. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is shown. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings.

Permissions

The Game asks for permission to vibrate the device, which it uses for short haptic feedback when you play; you can turn it off in the Game's settings. It also uses internet access and the advertising ID permission, for the ads described above. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with a new “last updated” date.
